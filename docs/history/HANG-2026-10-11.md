# The capture hang, and the rule it produced

Moved here verbatim on 2026-10-11, when the operator directed the hang be fixed
at its root and the rule below was rewritten in
[`../../TODO/RULES.md`](../../TODO/RULES.md) and
[`../AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md) absolute 16.

## What was believed

`capture-client` runs 3 to 34 showed *Install the client* running until the
job was cancelled, with no job log and no artifact. Thirteen bounds were added
to that step and none ended it. The conclusion recorded was that the hang was
unboundable and must not be diagnosed, and the rule below was written from it.

## What was measured on 2026-10-11

A flight recorder, `scripts/ci/flight-recorder.sh`, posted the host's state to a
check run every two seconds from the first step of the job. With markers
written before and after each operation of the install step:

- every operation of *Install the client* ran to exit 0, including the
  `ps` watchdog and `report-holders`, in seconds;
- the posts stopped the moment the next step, *Cut the route off this host*,
  deleted the default routes, and never resumed;
- with the capture moved into a network namespace and the host's routes left
  alone, the same job completed in two minutes with its log and its install
  artifact.

So nothing was ever hung. The containment cut the runner agent off from GitHub,
GitHub kept showing the last step it had heard about, and the host never came
back. Every bound was aimed at a step that had already finished.

⛔ **The rule turned a misreading into a prohibition on correcting it.** It
forbade the one thing that found the cause - an instrument that leaves the host
while the job runs - on the grounds that instruments left nothing, when what
they lacked was a way off the host.

## The rule, as it stood in `TODO/RULES.md`

## ⛔ A hang is a dead end, not a subject

⛔ **When something hangs, it is a DEAD END. Take a different route.** Do not
diagnose it, do not instrument it, and above all do not add a bound: record the
hang with its evidence, pick the alternative that does not depend on the hanging
thing, and go.

⚠ **This is a rule because it has been broken expensively.** `capture-client`
runs 3 to 31 spent **thirteen bounds** and more than a dozen dispatches on one
step that could not be ended - by `timeout` inside the script, by `timeout`
around it, by a privileged `timeout`, by a watchdog, by the job's own
`timeout-minutes`, and finally by GitHub's own step supervisor. Every one of them
failed the same way, and a cancelled job uploads nothing, so most of those
dispatches measured nothing at all.

⛔ **Two bounds is the limit.** If a second bound does not end it, the thing is
unboundable from where you are standing and no further bound will help. ⭐ The
question then is never *why does it hang* but **what path avoids it entirely** -
`ACQ-06` is what that question produced here, and it removes the privilege the
hang needed rather than measuring it.

⚠ **A hang that costs a dispatch costs more than it looks.** A run that is
cancelled leaves no log and no artifact, so the next reading is built on the
absence of evidence, which is where wrong causes get recorded as located ones.
